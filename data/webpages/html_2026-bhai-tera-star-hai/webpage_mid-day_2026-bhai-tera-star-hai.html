Bhai Tera Star Hai movie review: Raghav Juyal is more of a side character than hero

Updated On: 30 July, 2026 09:17 AM IST | Mumbai | Aakruti Bagla

Bhai Tera Star Hai Review: Raghav Juyal's first solo lead has a promising comic setup but suffers due to a convoluted screenplay, forced humour and weak execution. Despite energetic performances, the film turns into an exhausting watch

Listen to this article :04:09 min

Bhai Tera Star Hai review (Pic/YouTube)

After impressing audiences with his performances in Kill and The Bads of Bollywood, Raghav Juyal has steadily emerged as a promising actor. With Bhai Tera Star Hai, he steps into his first solo lead role. But does it deliver on its promise, or does the chaos outweigh the comedy? Let's find out.

Bhai Tera Star Hai follows the chaotic life of Ajay Singh, a struggling actor who is convinced he is already an established star. Reality, however, paints a different picture. He is confronted by Fatty (Sanjay Kapoor), a London-based bar owner who gives him just one-and-a-half hours to repay his debt. Instead of admitting the truth, Ajay confidently misleads Fatty by claiming he has bagged the role of Salman Khan's brother in Kisi Ka Bhai Kisi Ki Behen and that the payment is on its way.
